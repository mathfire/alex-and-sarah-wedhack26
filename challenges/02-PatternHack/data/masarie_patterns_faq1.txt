Am I missing anything? Is anything worded too technically or in an unclear way? THANK YOU LOVE YOU Annie 🧭 Getting Started What do you need from me to get started? We can start anywhere! A reference garment, rough sketch, or reference images are all great. It’s helpful to know your target customer, fabrics you're considering, and what pricepoint you’re aiming for, but if you’re unsure at this stage, no problem. Do I need to commit to a full package to get started? Nope! We only require a base size pattern and a basic tech pack order to begin the process. You can take the process step-by-step and only move to the next phase when you’re ready. All of the additional services we offer are a la carte and can be added to your order at any time. What if I already have a tech pack or don’t need one? If you already have a tech pack, we will happily review it to see if it falls within our quality requirements. We typically require an internal tech pack as our standard process includes creating and refining your technical drawing for approval before patterning begins. This step ensures we’re aligned on construction and design details from the get-go. If you only want a pattern, we do make exceptions in certain cases, so feel free to reach out with your specific needs and we’ll let you know what’s possible. Will you sign an NDA? Yes. We’re happy to sign an NDA before discussing your project. We accept standard NDA confidentiality agreements that state we will not share your designs, concepts, or project information with anyone outside our team. What software do you use? We work in industry‑standard CAD software for patternmaking and technical development including Optitex, Lectra, CLO 3D, and Adobe Illustrator. This ensures accuracy, consistency, and seamless communication with any manufacturer you choose. What pattern file type do you provide? Included with your pattern is a DXF - which is a universal format that can be read by any pattern software - and a PDF. Upon request, we can provide your pattern in almost any file type including, but not limited to: .mdl (Lectra), .zip (Gerber), .PDS (Optitex), .zprj (CLO 3D), HPG/HPGL/PLT (read by plotters), and .ai (Adobe Illustrator). Can I get a physical copy of my pattern? Absolutely! We can provide paper pattern copies on standard paper or oak tag (a heavier, more durable manilla paper). We also offer pattern tiling so you can print on your printer at home on US letter or legal size paper and tape the pieces together following match points and alignment codes. Can you help me find materials? Yes! We can provide you a list of trusted resources to explore if you want to give it a go on your own, or we can collect production available options for you and send you physical swatches to test and feel. 🧵 Understanding the Process What is the difference between a mock‑up, digital rendering, and sew‑by sample? A mock‑up is a quick physical draft to check shape and proportion. It is typically done in a substitute fabric, similar to the intended final fabric, but can be done in the final fabric if available. A digital rendering is a CAD visual that helps us align on design details before sewing. The garment is digitally sewn and shown on an avatar. This can be a helpful way to gauge shape and proportion with little environmental impact. A sew‑by sample is a fully constructed garment used to evaluate fit, construction, and production readiness. What’s the difference between a tech pack and a pattern? A pattern is the blueprint used to cut and sew your garment. A tech pack is the instruction manual for production that includes measurements, materials, construction notes, finishing details, and everything a factory needs to build your style correctly. Why do you offer multiple tech pack tiers? We offer multiple tech pack tiers because every brand is at a different stage and every style has different needs. Some clients are just getting started and want to keep things simple and budget‑friendly. Others are preparing for large‑scale production and need a fully built‑out, factory‑ready package. Our tiered system gives you the flexibility to start small, upgrade later, and choose only the level of detail your project truly requires. Each tier builds on the previous one, adding more clarity, documentation, and production support as you move up. This lets you scale your development at your own pace without paying for information you don’t need yet. You can view the full breakdown of each tier here: [Download: Tech Pack Tier Overview PDF] How do you determine how to grade my pattern? We grade based on your size range, your brand’s fit preferences, the proportion of the specific garment, and current industry standards. If your brand has a unique fit profile, we can customize the grade to align with it. How do fittings take place? Fittings can be done in person or virtually. During a fitting, we look at proportion, mobility, comfort, and construction details, then make targeted adjustments to refine the pattern. Not local to Nashville? Not a problem. We typically fit the garment on our end with our industry fit models with you virtually in attendance, then ship the sample to you to test and confirm agreed upon changes before proceeding with updates. ⏱️ Timelines & Expectations How long does the process take until I see my first prototype? Timelines depend on many factors. Once we review your project scope, we’ll give you a clear timeline so you know exactly what to expect. What is a typical timeline from initial concept to having production inventory? For most brands, the full journey including sourcing, development, sampling, revisions, matching with a manufacturer, and production, typically takes 8-10 months. Complexity, materials, and production volume can shift that slightly. How many rounds of sampling should I expect? Most styles take 1-3 samples to finalize fit and construction. More technical garments may need additional iterations, but we’ll guide you through each step. How many revisions can I make before I’m production-ready? As many as you like! Your first round of pattern and tech pack revisions are included in your initial price. For additional rounds of revisions, we’ll always let you know when a change may affect timeline or cost. Can I make updates to the tech pack myself? Yes, upon request we can release the working document to you to edit. However, we’re unable to provide additional updates or revisions to that file after it’s released. This helps us preserve the accuracy, formatting, and structure we’ve carefully built, and ensures the document stays consistent with our internal standards. If you need further changes beyond that point, we can always create an updated version as a new round of work. 💰 Pricing How much does it cost to develop one style? Pricing depends on complexity of the style, number of pattern pieces, and the level of detail involved. Once we see your design and understand your goals, we’ll send a detailed quote and pricing breakdown. When is payment due? A 50% deposit based on the low end estimate is required to get started with the balance due upon completion. Project delivery is contingent on payment in full. A 1.8% per month late fee will apply to balances unpaid after a 7-day grace period. 🏭 Production Do you do production in house? No, we don’t do production manufacturing at this time. When you’re ready, we will happily connect you with our trusted manufacturing partners both domestic and overseas. 📄 Ownership & Rights Do I own my patterns? You receive unlimited, perpetual usage rights to all patterns created for your brand. This means you can manufacture, reproduce, modify, and scale them freely with any factory. The underlying intellectual property remains with the studio, which is standard across the industry. <learn more link> the sub menu for “learn more” What does “unlimited usage rights” mean? You can use your patterns and tech packs for: Any production run Any manufacturer Any future season Any quantity Any modifications you choose to make There are no restrictions on how your brand uses the patterns or tech packs. Why doesn’t full ownership automatically transfer? Pattern intellectual property functions similarly to photography or graphic design: The creator retains the underlying IP unless it is formally assigned. This protects the studio’s internal methods, drafting systems, and proprietary workflows. You still receive everything needed to operate your brand without limitation. Can the studio reuse my patterns for other clients? No. Your pattern and tech pack is specific to your brand and your product. The studio only retains the underlying drafting IP, not your design. Why is this structure standard in the industry? Because patternmaking involves proprietary drafting systems, internal blocks, and technical methods developed over years. Studios retain IP to protect their methodology while giving clients full operational freedom. In the apparel industry, full pattern IP transfer is extremely uncommon. Most garments share similar functional features such as seams, shaping methods, and construction techniques, so no studio can agree to restrictions that limit the ability to create patterns or tech packs for similar products in the future. That would prevent us from serving other clients and developing standard functional garments. In very special cases, like when a garment includes a completely new functional feature or a truly unseen design, full IP transfer can be discussed as a separate option. It requires a formal assignment agreement and an additional fee, and it’s generally not recommended unless the design is truly novel.
