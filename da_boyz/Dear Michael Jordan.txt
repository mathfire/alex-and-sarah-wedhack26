Subject: A Completely Reasonable 2026 Wedding Request

Dear Mr. Jordan and the Jump Management Team,

My name is Alex Masarie. I’m getting married to Sarah Ruiz in New Mexico on October 11, 2026, and I realize there is an extremely small chance this email goes anywhere beyond your inbox.

I’m sending it anyway.

I was born in 1987, and Michael Jordan was one of the first larger-than-life heroes I had as a kid. When he retired the first time, I remember watching the coverage, wearing my #23 jersey, and feeling like something enormous had just happened to the world.

That stayed with me.

So naturally, now that I’m getting married, I’ve done something completely reasonable: I’ve built a ridiculous racquetball event into the wedding.

It’s called the Groomsmen Gauntlet. A handful of friends will enter the court for predetermined single-volley matches against me, the Bridegroom, with their defeats becoming increasingly theatrical. It’s part racquetball, part theater, and entirely ridiculous.

I would love to invite Mr. Jordan into that world in whatever small way might be possible.

A brief “Congratulations, Alex and Sarah” message would mean a great deal to me. A short pre recorded video would be incredible. And in the most statistically improbable version of this story:

Mr. Jordan arrives at 7:09 PM and the entire wedding loses its mind!

I understand that the odds of that are approximately astronomical. I also understand that this is an unusual request, which is precisely why I wanted to make it rather than spend the rest of my life wondering whether I should have.

There is absolutely no expectation attached to this invitation. If nothing comes of it, I’m still grateful that I even got to send it out.

But if there is some small way for His Airness to become part of this ridiculous wedding story, I would be genuinely honored.

With enormous respect,

Alex Masarie
