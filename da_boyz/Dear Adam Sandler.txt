Subject: A Completely Reasonable 2026 Wedding Request

Dear WME / Team Sandler,

My name is Alex Masarie, and I’m getting married to Sarah Ruiz in New Mexico on October 11.  This is either going to be the strangest wedding invitation received this year or proof that the internet has finally gone too far.

And I realize this email is probably going to sound ridiculous.  That’s okay. Our wedding is ridiculous... in a very intentional way.

We’ve built a little world around it called **WedHack26**—part wedding, part community experiment, part art project, and part increasingly unreasonable list of people I’ve decided should somehow be involved.

Mr. Sandler is on that list for a very specific reason:

If Taylor and Travis can have Adam Sandler involved in their 2026 wedding universe, I have decided that Alex and Sarah should at least ask.

One piece of this is the Groomsmen Gauntlet, a racquetball event in which a succession of brave men will enter the court at our venue in Tijeras, New Mexico for the express purpose of losing to me, the Bridegroom, in increasingly ridiculous fashion. We're talking overt Bridegroom cheating, wardrobe malfunctions, racquet launches, through-the-legs shots, questionable officiating, and — for reasons still unclear — a salami sandwich.

I am not asking Mr. Sandler to attend. I am not asking for a performance. I am not even asking you to take this seriously.

But if there is some universe in which Adam Sandler could send us a short “Congratulations, Alex and Sarah” video or note—or contribute some other tiny bit of Sandler-shaped magic to the wedding—I would be absolutely delighted.

And if not, no worries whatsoever.

The fact that I got to write this email is already pretty damn funny.
